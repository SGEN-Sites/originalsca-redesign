COMPLETE GUIDE TO BUYING ORIGINALS WEED AT ORIGINALS SAN DIEGO

ORIGINALS • June 20, 2021

The second official retailer of Originals has just opened at 6220 Federal Blvd in sunny San Diego this past Cinco de Mayo. Those in search of award-winning genetics and the most cutting-edge cannabis products will find our new dispensary a welcoming home. Alongside our own popular flower products, you’ll find the same brands and innovators we carry at Originals Factory & Weed Shop in LA.

Originals is a family-owned business of LA natives that’s been serving the Los Angeles community high-quality cannabis since 2006. Quality control is our biggest concern, and it shows from the menu we curate to the flower we cultivate. The relationships we’ve built over the past decade and a half come to life on our lavish menu. Many of the celebrities working in cannabis have become our friends over the years and keep us in good supply.

Open seven days a week from 10 am-9 pm, and closing at 8 on Sundays, we cater to the community with accessibility. Customers can feel at home in our spacious and sleek new showroom that features numerous display cases of California’s best weed products. Ask a budtender about what the freshest flower or most popular edibles are and trust that you’re in good hands. Consider ordering online through our Weedmaps page in order to skip the line.

The Freshest Flower In SD

At Originals San Diego, you’ll find an artisanal curation of California’s premium products alongside our name brands. We’re keeping the freshest cuts of our in-house strains grown indoors at our Originals Weed Factory & Weed Shop in LA. You can purchase our premium cannabis flower in eighths. More cost-conscious customers can now also find our Originals Small Buds representing all of our same sought-after strains at a more affordable price. Current Small Buds strains include Wedding Cake, Oakstradam OG, London Pound Cake, Originals OG, and Divorce Cake x Zkittlez Cake.

Premium Eighths

The following strains are available in premium eighths from Originals: Gelato, Animal Face, Oakstradam OG, Wedding Cake, and Originals OG. Read more below about each of these strains and understand where we get our name. These are the original cuts of some of the most popular indica strains on the West Coast.

Gelato

Originals’ Gelato is true to the legend this hybrid strain has built. A cross between Sunset Sherbet and Thin Mint GSC, this strain has risen into infamy for its potentially euphoric highs and heavily sedative effects. As the name suggests, this strain reeks of dessert-like aromas that come across sweet, fruity, and spicy. Rich purple and green hues decorate the nugs with fiery orange pistils interwoven that offer illuminating contrast. The resinous layer of trichomes coating its surface shimmer like crystals. A THC powerhouse, Gelato has become a favorite among creatives that claim it may provide a strong physical high. Medical users might find the Gelato strain to be ideal for combating insomnia, fatigue, and chronic pain.

Animal Face

The Animal Face strain by Originals is a rare sativa-dominant OG Kush variety that takes on more of a piney and gassy terpene profile with cookie undertones. By crossing Face-Off OG with Animal Mints, you end up with this energizing hitter. This strain comes from the geniuses at Seed Junky Genetics. It is known for producing potentially body-numbing and mood-lifting highs. The nugs are dense with light and dark green coloration, showing off a glittering coating of trichomes on its surface. Drawing from famous Californian genetics, this strain may hit hard like an OG at first, but leave you functional enough to still get things done. Our Animal Face strain might also be useful for anyone dealing with inflammation, nausea, or mental and physical stress.

Oakstradam OG

Originals’ Oakstradam OG strain is a celebrity cut of OG Kush developed by Oaksterdam University in Oakland. The origins of this strain are said to be from crossing a NorCal strain with a Hindu Kush plant from Amsterdam. This heavy indica exhibits all of the classic characteristics of a potent OG taken to the extreme. Wildly dense buds show off deep green hues with dark red pistils. A thick blanket of resinous trichomes covers its surface, holding the nugs together like glue. Originals’ Oakstradam OG is known for its loud peppery, sweet, and musky flavors that might promote stress relief and create a sense of stoney euphoria.

Wedding Cake

The Originals Wedding Cake strain may turn any day into a special occasion. A potent indica-hybrid strain from Seed Junky Genetics, this beauty comes from crossing Triangle Kush with Animal Mints. Pale and dark green with ripe orange pistils, each nug has a frosty layer of trichomes. On the nose, you’ll notice tart vanilla aromas with hoppy undertones. If the terps don’t get you, the high THC content of our Wedding Cake strain is sure to leave a lasting impression. Smoking some of our Wedding Cake may uplift your mind and put your body at ease. This strain might be a welcoming companion to anyone suffering from depression, anxiety, or stress.

Originals OG

The Mac Daddy of OG Kush varieties, the Originals OG is some serious kush. You’ll notice an incredibly sweet lemon aroma on the nose. As well as its brilliant green foliage that has curly orange pistils poking out. Dense to the touch, the Originals OG has a short, yet pointy appearance that dully glimmers from the yellowed trichomes that wrap its surface. Those looking for the iconic calm that OG’s may provide will delight in this strain. The sweetness from the aroma still comes out amidst a mildly spicy smoke. Medical may find this strain to be a consistent cut of OG for anxiety relief.
